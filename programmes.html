Programmes

Four Programmes,
One Pathway.

From first touches at nine years old to competitive adult football. Whatever the age or level, there is a place to start.

01

Foundation

U11 · Ages 9-11

Our entry point, where young players fall in love with the game and build the coordination everything else is layered on.

Who It Is For

Boys and girls aged 9 to 11, with or without any organised football experience.

Development Focus

First touch, balance, coordination and confidence. Enjoyment is the measure of success.

What Players Learn

Ball mastery, dribbling, simple passing and receiving, and how to be a good teammate.

Training Approach

Short, high-energy sessions built on small-sided games. Lots of touches, very little queueing.

Match Opportunities

Internal small-sided games and friendly festivals, with equal playing time for every child.

02

Youth

U14 · Ages 12-14

The phase where raw ability becomes real technique, and players begin to understand the game as well as play it.

Who It Is For

Players aged 12 to 14 ready to train consistently on a structured weekly programme.

Development Focus

Technical repetition under pressure, positional understanding and physical literacy.

What Players Learn

Passing range, first touch under pressure, one-v-one play, positional roles and decision making.

Training Approach

Themed weekly blocks with clear objectives. Every player is paired with a mentor.

Match Opportunities

Competitive fixtures against academies and school sides around Ibadan, plus tournaments.

03

Development

U17 · Ages 15-17

Our most competitive youth programme, for players who are serious about how far football can take them.

Who It Is For

Committed players aged 15 to 17 ready for 11-a-side football and demanding coaching.

Development Focus

Game intelligence, tactical detail, conditioning and preparation for senior football.

What Players Learn

Advanced positional play, pressing and defensive structure, set pieces, professional habits.

Training Approach

Higher-volume training with video review and individual plans, built around school.

Match Opportunities

A full 11-a-side fixture programme, showcase matches and exposure to trial opportunities.

04

Amateur

Ages 18+

Our senior side. The next step for graduating players and an open door for adult footballers across Ibadan.

Who It Is For

Adults aged 18 and over, including academy graduates and walk-on local players.

Development Focus

Competitive performance, fitness, team structure and leadership on and off the pitch.

What Players Learn

Senior tactical systems, game management, and the discipline of competing as a squad.

Training Approach

Evening training designed around work and study, with emphasis on conditioning.

Match Opportunities

Competitive local league and cup football representing Limitless Reach at senior level.
