How to Join

Three Steps
to a Place.

Step One

Register Your Interest

Whether you are registering yourself or your child, tell us the age and the programme of interest. It takes two minutes and costs nothing.

Step Two

Attend an Open Trial

We confirm the date and venue by email. Trials are free to attend, and we will let you know the outcome afterwards.

Join Your Programme

Selected players join their squad for the January 2027 opening, with fees and schedules confirmed in advance.
