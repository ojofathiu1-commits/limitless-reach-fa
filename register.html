By registering for a trial, I confirm that the information provided in this form is accurate to the best of my knowledge and that the player is medically fit to participate, except for any conditions or injuries disclosed above.

For players under 18, I give permission for my child to participate in the Limitless Reach Football Academy trial and associated football activities.

Emergency Medical Consent
