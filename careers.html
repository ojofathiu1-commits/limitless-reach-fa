Youth & Development Coach

All ages · Ibadan

  • Available evenings and weekends

03 Recruitment

Scout / Recruitment Analyst

Pathway · Ibadan

Identify talented players across Ibadan and Oyo State, watch local fixtures and trials, and build clear player reports that support selection.

  • A sharp eye for potential
  • Strong local football network
  • Clear written reporting

04 Operations

Club Administrator & Operations Coordinator

Academy-wide · Ibadan

Manage registrations, schedules, facilities, kit and day-to-day communication with families and players so everything runs smoothly.

  • Highly organised
  • Confident communicator

05 Welfare

Welfare & Safeguarding Officer

Academy-wide · Ibadan

Lead our safeguarding policy, run background checks and be the trusted first point of contact for player and family welfare.

  • Safeguarding training or background
  • Calm, discreet and approachable
  • Works well with parents and staff
