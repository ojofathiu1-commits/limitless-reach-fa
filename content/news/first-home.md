Every academy needs a place where players can arrive, settle in and get to work. For Limitless Reach, that first home is taking shape in Bodija, Ibadan.

We are focused on establishing a training base that serves the whole academy: younger players taking their first steps, teenagers who want to compete, and adult players looking for a serious football environment.

## Why a Home Base Matters

A consistent base gives players more than a pitch. It gives families a reliable meeting point, coaches a place to plan and teams the routine that helps good habits stick. It is where players learn to arrive prepared, look after the space and represent something bigger than themselves.

The search is centred on Bodija so the academy can be part of the local community it serves. Our aim is a practical, welcoming place where training can happen regularly and safely.

## Building for the Long Term

- **Regular training.** A reliable base for structured sessions throughout the week.
- **Space to grow.** A home that can support every programme as the academy develops.
- **Community connection.** A place for players, families and partners to build lasting relationships.

> The first pitch is where a young player begins to see a pathway in front of them.

## Follow the Progress

We will share further updates as the base is confirmed. In the meantime, players can register for trials and local organisations or potential partners can contact us to start a conversation.
