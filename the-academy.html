The Academy

More Than a
Football Academy.

Limitless Reach is a football development academy in Ibadan created to help players grow through quality coaching, strong values and meaningful opportunities to progress. We provide a structured environment where players can improve their game, build confidence and develop the character needed to succeed on and off the pitch.

Why Limitless Reach Exists

Talent Deserves
Structure.

Ibadan has always produced talented footballers. What many players lack is consistent access to quality coaching, regular competition, honest feedback and clear development pathways. Limitless Reach was created to help close that gap. We believe players in Oyo State should have the opportunity to develop in a professional, supportive environment without having to depend on luck or connections.

Our Purpose

Our Purpose Is Simple.

Develop

Build Better Players

We provide structured coaching that develops technical ability, tactical understanding, decision-making and physical capability.

Unlock

Create Real Opportunities

We create access to competitive football, trials, showcases and progression opportunities that allow players to test themselves and take the next step when they are ready.

Build

Create Something That Lasts

We are building an academy rooted in Ibadan, connected to its community and committed to developing players for years to come.

Our Philosophy

The Roots.
The Mind. The Reach.

Everything we do is built around three connected pillars. We believe strong players are developed from the inside out: first through character, then through mindset, and finally through the opportunity to stretch their ability.

01

The Roots

Character. Discipline. Belonging. Development starts with who you are. We teach players to be respectful, humble, disciplined and responsible. We want every player to feel part of something, represent the academy well and remain grounded as they progress.

02

The
 Mind

Confidence. Focus. Resilience. Football challenges the mind as much as the body. We help players build confidence, stay focused, respond positively to setbacks and develop the habits required to improve consistently.

03

The Reach

Coaching. Development. Opportunity. This is where potential is stretched. Through purposeful training, competitive football and clear progression opportunities, we help players improve and reach the highest level their ability and commitment can take them.

What We Stand For

Standards That Shape
Everything We Do.

01

Respect

We respect teammates, coaches, opponents, officials, families and the game itself.

02

Discipline

We prepare properly, work consistently and maintain high standards.

03

Humility

04

Hard Work

Our Mission

To provide high-quality football development that builds ability, confidence and character in equal measure.

Our Ambition

To become one of the most trusted football development environments in Oyo State, creating a pathway from grassroots football in Ibadan to the highest level each player can reach.

Player Welfare & Safeguarding

A Safe Place to
Learn, Play and Grow.

The welfare of every player comes first. We are committed to creating an environment where children and adults feel safe, respected and supported at every training session, match and academy activity.

Vetted Coaches & Staff

Relevant staff are appropriately screened, referenced and prepared before working with players.

Dedicated Safeguarding Lead

A named Safeguarding Lead provides a clear point of contact for players, parents and staff.

Parental Consent

Players under 18 participate with appropriate parental or guardian consent, with emergency and relevant medical information securely maintained.

Codes of Conduct

Players, coaches, staff and parents are expected to follow clear standards of behaviour, communication and respect.

First Aid & Injury Management

Appropriate first-aid arrangements and clear injury reporting procedures are maintained across academy activities.

Raising a Concern

Players and families will always have a confidential route for raising safeguarding or welfare concerns: operations@limitlessreachfa.com
